How KoraTask works

A task owner posts a task and sets a budget. The platform calculates a 20% fee. In this first version, payments are simulated only; do not enter real card or mobile-money credentials.
1. Post a task → 2. Worker applies → 3. Task is completed → 4. Owner approves → 5. Worker receives 80% in the demo wallet.
